ABOUT MECTROZ'26

MECTROZ'26 is an International Student Symposium organised by the Department of Mechanical Engineering and Mechanical Engineering Association of EGS Pillay Engineering College.


The symposium brings together students to share ideas, demonstrate technical skills and explore emerging engineering technologies.

CORE TOPICS

Thermodynamics Fluid Mechanics Machine Design Manufacturing Automotive Engineering Robotics CAD Modelling
