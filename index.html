ABOUT THE SYMPOSIUM

ENGINEERING THE FUTURE

MECTROZ'26

MECTROZ'26 is an International Student Symposium organized by the Department of Mechanical Engineering, EGS Pillay Engineering College.


The symposium brings together students, innovators and engineering enthusiasts to explore emerging technologies, sustainable innovation and intelligent mechanical systems.
